# 📱 Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze mobile sales data and provide meaningful insights into sales performance, revenue, transactions, products, customers, payment methods, and sales trends.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into interactive visualizations and KPIs that can help businesses monitor performance and identify important sales patterns.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze mobile sales data and answer important business questions such as:

* What is the overall sales performance?
* What is the total revenue generated?
* How many mobile transactions were completed?
* Which mobile brands and models perform best?
* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* How do sales vary over time?
* Which locations generate the most sales?
* Which payment methods are most commonly used?
* What are the major customer and sales trends?
* How can sales performance be monitored through an interactive dashboard?
